How it works

  1. Build your site

    Put your website on GitHub Pages. It's free, and you don't need to have built a website before. The guide walks you through it.

  2. Verify you're a student

    Sign in with GitHub and confirm your school email with a code. Your email stays private.

  3. Claim your name

    Open a request on GitHub. Our bot checks it in a minute, and a person approves it. Then your address is live.

Questions

Does it cost anything?
No. The address is free, and so is hosting on GitHub Pages. Nobody will ask you for payment details.
Who can join?
Students aged 13 or older at a high school, college or university, with a GitHub account that's at least 30 days old.
I don't have a school email address.
You can still verify. See verifying without a school email.
What happens to my email address?
We use it once to send you a code. We don't store it, publish it or share it. The privacy page has the details.
What can I put on my site?
Anything that's yours and legit: portfolios, projects, blogs, art, clubs. No scams, phishing, impersonation or harmful content. Read the rules.
