Rules

The short version. The full Terms of Service are on GitHub.

Who can join

  • Students aged 13 or older at a high school, college or university
  • With a GitHub account at least 30 days old, verified on the verify page
  • One name per person

What's welcome

Portfolios, resumes, blogs, school and personal projects, art, clubs and teams, open-source work. If it's yours and it's legit, it belongs here.

What's not allowed

  • Phishing, scams, or pages that ask for passwords or payment details
  • Malware or anything that harms visitors
  • Pretending to be another person, school, company or brand
  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate
  • Adult content, or anything illegal
  • Selling, renting or sitting on names without a real site

Names that break these rules are removed, and the account may be blocked from has-a.tech.

Keep it running

Keep your site online and your file up to date. Names that point at nothing for a long time may be removed.

Report a problem

If you see a has-a.tech address being misused, email abuse@has-a.tech. We read every report.

No guarantees

has-a.tech is free and run by volunteers. We do our best, but we can't promise it will always be available.
