No school email?

Some schools don't give students an email address, or use one we don't recognize yet.

  • If your school has an email domain we don't know, ask us to add it. Don't include your own email address in the request.
  • If you have no school email at all, email verify@has-a.tech from any address with your GitHub username and your school's name. We'll reply with how to confirm, and a person checks it by hand.

If we ask for proof such as a photo of a student card, cover everything except your name, your school and the school year: no student number, photo or date of birth. We delete it as soon as we've checked, and it's never posted anywhere. Never put it in a GitHub issue or pull request.
